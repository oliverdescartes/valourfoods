Scroll Story System

Dual scroll spotlight for premium product storytelling.

The copy flows at reading speed while the pinned media surface performs a continuous, scroll-scrubbed handoff between product moments.

01

Quiet materials, exact edges.

A calm opening moment introduces the product language with soft contrast, restrained depth, and a surface that feels engineered rather than decorated.

02

A profile that rises into view.

The next visual does not replace the first abruptly. It arrives from below while the previous frame lifts away, keeping the visual rhythm unbroken.

03

Details hold just long enough.

Each image settles in the center for a short beat, giving the viewer time to register the detail before the sequence continues upward.

04

Motion stays physically consistent.

The entire sequence uses transform and opacity only, preserving compositing performance and avoiding layout work during scroll.

05

A polished final reveal.

The last frame completes the chained climb with the same timing model as every previous transition, so there are no gaps, flashes, or sudden swaps.

End State

One timeline. One continuous scroll performance.

The media never toggles on or off. Every frame exists in the stack, and the scroll position simply drives the shared choreography.
